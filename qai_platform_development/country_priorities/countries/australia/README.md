# Australia Country Priority Profile

Working country profile for QAI Platform Development, product alignment and gap analysis.

This profile provides the Australia-specific layer connecting Australian national and sector priorities to the common priority taxonomy, potential product opportunities, platform capabilities and development gaps.

It is an analytical working profile, not a ranking of Australian priorities or sectors.

~~~

## 1. Purpose

The Australia Country Priority Profile provides a structured entry point for analysing how the QAI Platform and its products may align with Australian national, sector and technology priorities.

The profile follows:

```text
Australian Source / Evidence
        ↓
National Priority
        ↓
Common Priority Area
        ↓
Sector
        ↓
Problem / Opportunity
        ↓
Product Alignment
        ↓
Platform Capability
        ↓
Gap Analysis
        ↓
Development Requirement
```

The profile separates documented Australian priorities from opportunities inferred for QAI Platform Development.

~~~

## 3. Evidence Principle

The Australia profile should distinguish between:

### Documented Priority

A priority explicitly stated by an Australian Government or other authoritative source.

### Documented Sector Application

A source explicitly connects a priority or technology to a sector.

### Analytical Inference

A sector, problem or opportunity inferred from documented evidence.

### Product Opportunity

A potential QAI product or service identified through analysis.

These categories should not be presented as equivalent.

```text
Official Evidence
      ↓
Documented Priority
      ↓
Documented / Inferred Sector
      ↓
Analytical Opportunity
      ↓
Potential Product
```

~~~

## 18. Country-Specific vs Common Capabilities

The Australia profile should distinguish:

### Common Capability

A capability potentially reusable across Canada, India, Australia and other markets.

### Australia-Specific Capability

A capability requiring adaptation to:

- Australian regulation
- Australian data
- Australian infrastructure
- Australian industry processes
- Australian geography
- Australian customer requirements
- Australian government programs
- Australian security requirements

```text
Common Platform Capability
        ↓
Australia Adaptation
        ↓
Australian Product / Use Case
```

~~~
