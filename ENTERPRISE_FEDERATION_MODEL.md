# Overview

The Enterprise Federation Model (EFM) defines how the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem collaborates with external organizations while maintaining enterprise governance, security, operational autonomy and digital sovereignty.

Unlike traditional enterprise architectures that assume centralized ownership, the HoldCo architecture recognizes that many operational environments are collaborative ecosystems involving governments, universities, research organizations, industry partners, customers and international alliances.

The Enterprise Federation Model establishes the architectural principles that enable secure, scalable and interoperable federation across these independent domains.

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Federation Model establishes:

- Enterprise Ownership Model
- Federation Architecture
- Federation Governance
- Trust Boundaries
- Digital Sovereignty
- Enterprise Collaboration
- Shared Runtime Architecture
- Federated Operations

The objective is to enable enterprise growth through collaboration rather than centralized ownership.

# Enterprise Principle

The HoldCo ecosystem follows a fundamental architectural principle:

> **HoldCo owns enterprises. HoldCo federates ecosystems.**

Enterprise ownership and ecosystem participation are intentionally separated.

---

# Enterprise Ownership Model

The HoldCo architecture distinguishes between owned enterprise assets and federated operational ecosystems.

## Owned Enterprise Domains

Owned domains are governed directly by HoldCo.

Examples include:

- HoldCo
- Subsidiaries
- Enterprise Architecture
- Enterprise Operating Platform
- Enterprise Frameworks
- Enterprise Registries
- Product Portfolio
- Product Foundry
- Enterprise Factory
- QAI Laboratory
- Technology Transfer Office
- Corporate Affairs
- Enterprise Intellectual Property

These domains remain under direct enterprise governance.

---

## Federated Domains

Federated domains are independently governed organizations or ecosystems that collaborate with HoldCo.

Examples include:

- Digital Valleys
- Smart Cities
- National Programs
- Government Agencies
- Universities
- Research Institutes
- Industry Partners
- Startup Communities
- Customer Enterprises
- International Collaborations
- Space Programs

Federated domains retain operational autonomy while interoperating through common enterprise standards.
