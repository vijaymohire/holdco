# Overview

The Enterprise Operating Platform (EOP) defines the operational architecture responsible for managing, coordinating and evolving the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Unlike traditional enterprise management platforms that focus on infrastructure administration, the Enterprise Operating Platform coordinates Enterprise Workspaces, Capability Packages, Enterprise Services, Runtime Platforms, Control Planes and Operational Ecosystems into a unified operating environment.

The platform enables software-defined enterprise operations capable of supporting Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) environments across cloud, edge, industrial and future quantum infrastructure.
