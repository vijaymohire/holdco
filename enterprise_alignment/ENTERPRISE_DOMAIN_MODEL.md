**Status:** Work in Progress  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Repository:** HoldCo  
**Architecture Level:** Enterprise Domain Architecture (EDA)

---

# Overview

The Enterprise Domain Model (EDM) defines how the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem is partitioned into logical business, engineering, operational and technology domains.

Rather than organizing the enterprise solely through organizational hierarchies or repositories, the Enterprise Domain Model groups related capabilities into reusable domains that can evolve independently while remaining interoperable through common enterprise architecture principles.

Enterprise Domains provide the foundation for scalable enterprise composition, distributed governance and autonomous operations.

---

The Enterprise Domain Model establishes:

# Why Enterprise Domains?

As enterprises grow, organizational structures, technologies and products continuously evolve.

Enterprise Domains provide a stable organizational model by grouping related enterprise capabilities under common responsibilities.

Domains remain relatively stable even when implementations, technologies and organizational structures change.

# Domain Principles

Enterprise Domains follow several principles.

## Business Aligned

Domains reflect enterprise responsibilities.

---

## Capability Driven

Domains organize capabilities rather than technologies.

---

## Loosely Coupled

Domains minimize unnecessary dependencies.

---

## Highly Cohesive

Related capabilities remain together.

---

## Independently Evolvable

Domains evolve independently while remaining interoperable.

---

## Discoverable

Enterprise metadata enables domain discovery.

---

## Governed

Domains operate under enterprise governance.
