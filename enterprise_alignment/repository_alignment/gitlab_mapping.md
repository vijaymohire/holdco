# Overview

The GitLab Workspace Mapping documents how existing GitLab workspaces contribute to the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

These workspaces represent several years of enterprise architecture, research, engineering, product development and operational design. They contain valuable architectural knowledge that forms the foundation of the evolving HoldCo Enterprise Architecture.

Rather than replacing these workspaces, HoldCo aligns them into a unified Enterprise Capability Architecture and Enterprise Reference Model.

---

# Purpose

GitLab Workspace Mapping provides:

- Workspace inventory
- Enterprise capability mapping
- Repository relationships
- Historical architecture preservation
- Migration guidance
- Enterprise alignment
- Future evolution roadmap

The objective is to preserve architectural knowledge while enabling gradual modernization.

# GitLab Workspace Philosophy

GitLab workspaces represent enterprise engineering assets.
