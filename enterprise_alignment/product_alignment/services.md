# Overview

Enterprise Services define the professional, operational and managed services delivered by the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

While Enterprise Products provide reusable technology platforms, Enterprise Services apply those platforms to solve business challenges, accelerate customer transformation and deliver measurable operational outcomes.

Enterprise Services integrate Enterprise Capabilities, Enterprise Products, Engineering Frameworks and Runtime Services into complete customer engagements.

I think this is a very important document because it completes the transition from Products to Customer Value.

One distinction I'd recommend making is:

Products = Deliver technology.
Services = Deliver business outcomes using those products.

Looking back over your architecture (QAI Hub, FAEP Academy, Client Lab, Factory, Valley, Consulting, Modernization, Applied Research, etc.), HoldCo is really a Product + Services Enterprise.

So Services should not be treated as "support". They are an equal architectural layer.
