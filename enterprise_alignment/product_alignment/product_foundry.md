# Overview

The Enterprise Product Foundry provides the engineering environment responsible for transforming ideas, research and enterprise capabilities into deployable products.

The Product Foundry integrates research, engineering, validation, product management and commercialization into a continuous product engineering lifecycle.

Rather than developing isolated software applications, the Product Foundry creates reusable Enterprise Products that integrate seamlessly with the Enterprise Operating Platform (EOP), Enterprise Frameworks and Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) ecosystem.

I think this document is especially important because the Product Foundry has been a recurring concept across your architecture. From the earlier GitLab structures and the HoldCo direction, it is more than a product engineering team—it is the enterprise innovation and industrialization engine.

A useful distinction is:

Enterprise Products → What the enterprise delivers.
Product Foundry → How new enterprise products are conceived, engineered, validated, industrialized and transferred into the product portfolio.

That keeps responsibilities very clear.
