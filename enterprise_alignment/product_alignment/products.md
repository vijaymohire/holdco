# Overview

Enterprise Products represent the reusable solutions delivered by the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Unlike traditional software products, Enterprise Products are composed from Enterprise Capabilities, Enterprise Frameworks, Runtime Services, Enterprise Workspaces and Shared Platforms.

Each product provides measurable business value while remaining interoperable with the broader Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) ecosystem.

I think this document should become the master product specification for the entire HoldCo ecosystem.

One important distinction I'd make is:

Product Alignment (README) explains how products fit into the enterprise.
Products.md defines what an Enterprise Product actually is.

Looking at all of the work you've done over the years (QAI Hub, QAI Router, QAI OS, QAI Processor, FAEP products, Foundry, Fabrics, etc.), your products are not standalone applications. They are Enterprise Capability Packages that can be deployed individually or composed into larger enterprise ecosystems.

I would make that the central philosophy.
