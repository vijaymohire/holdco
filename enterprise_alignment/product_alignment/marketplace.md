# Overview

The Enterprise Marketplace provides the commercial interface between the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem and its customers, partners and collaborators.

Rather than functioning as a traditional software marketplace, the Enterprise Marketplace enables customers to discover, evaluate, acquire and compose Enterprise Products, Enterprise Services, Enterprise Frameworks and Enterprise Knowledge Assets into complete business solutions.

The Marketplace serves as the commercial gateway to the Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) ecosystem.

I think this is an excellent document to end the Product Alignment section because it represents the point where all enterprise capabilities become externally consumable.

One distinction I'd make is:

Products → What we build.
Services → How we deliver.
Marketplace → How customers discover, acquire, compose and consume our offerings.

The Marketplace should therefore be viewed as the Enterprise Commerce Layer, not merely an online store.
