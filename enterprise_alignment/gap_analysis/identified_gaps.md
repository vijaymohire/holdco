> **Enterprise Gap Register for the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem**

**Status:** Work in Progress  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Repository:** HoldCo  
**Architecture Level:** Enterprise Assessment

---

# Overview

The Enterprise Gap Register identifies architectural, operational, engineering and organizational opportunities for improving the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Rather than documenting failures, this register records enterprise capabilities that require additional maturity, integration, implementation or governance to achieve the long-term vision defined by the Master Enterprise Reference Architecture (MERA).

The register provides a living roadmap for continuous enterprise evolution.

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Gap Register establishes:

- Enterprise Capability Gaps
- Architecture Gaps
- Technology Gaps
- Governance Gaps
- Operational Gaps
- Integration Gaps
- Automation Gaps
- Federation Gaps
- Research Opportunities
- Future Roadmap Priorities

The objective is to maintain a continuously evolving enterprise improvement backlog.

---

# Assessment Philosophy

Enterprise maturity is achieved through continuous improvement.

Each identified gap represents one or more of the following:

- Missing Capability
- Partial Implementation
- Future Enhancement
- Technology Opportunity
- Process Improvement
- Architectural Refinement
- Operational Optimization

Gap identification supports strategic planning rather than defect reporting.
