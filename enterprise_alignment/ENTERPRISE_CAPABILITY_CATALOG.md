# Overview

The Enterprise Capability Catalog (ECC) provides the authoritative inventory of enterprise capabilities within the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

It serves as the enterprise-wide registry describing the business, engineering, operational and technology capabilities available across repositories, workspaces, subsidiaries, operational platforms and customer solutions.

Unlike implementation-specific documentation, the Enterprise Capability Catalog focuses on reusable enterprise capabilities that remain stable as technologies, organizational structures and implementation approaches evolve.

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Capability Catalog provides:

- Enterprise Capability Registry
- Capability Classification
- Capability Ownership
- Capability Relationships
- Capability Dependencies
- Capability Mapping
- Capability Lifecycle
- Capability Discoverability
- Capability Reuse

The catalog establishes a common enterprise vocabulary that supports consistent architectural decision-making.

# Why an Enterprise Capability Catalog?

Large enterprises often develop similar capabilities independently.

The Enterprise Capability Catalog reduces duplication by providing a centralized inventory of enterprise capabilities.

The catalog enables:

- Capability reuse
- Enterprise discoverability
- Cross-domain collaboration
- Architectural consistency
- Faster enterprise development
- Better governance
