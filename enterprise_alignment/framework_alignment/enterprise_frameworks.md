# Overview

Enterprise Frameworks define the strategic, organizational and operational methodologies that guide the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Rather than focusing on individual technologies or engineering disciplines, Enterprise Frameworks establish the reusable models that govern enterprise architecture, organizational design, capability management, operations, innovation and Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) transformation.

They provide the foundation upon which Engineering Frameworks, Enterprise Workspaces, Enterprise Operating Platform (EOP) and Enterprise Capabilities are built.

This document should become the highest-level framework document in the Framework Alignment section.

A distinction I recommend making is:

Engineering Frameworks → How we engineer systems.
Enterprise Frameworks → How we organize and operate the enterprise.

Enterprise Frameworks sit above Engineering Frameworks and provide the governance, architecture, organizational models, operating models, and strategic direction for the entire HoldCo ecosystem.
