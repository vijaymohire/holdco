# Overview

Governance Frameworks establish the decision-making, oversight and control mechanisms that guide the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

They define how enterprise strategy, architecture, engineering, operations, research, education and Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) initiatives are governed across multiple organizational levels.

Rather than relying on a single governance model, HoldCo adopts a layered governance architecture where strategic, operational and technical governance work together to support enterprise objectives.

From everything we've developed (HoldCo, Factory, Enterprise Operating Platform, Control Planes, Valleys, Cities, FAEP, etc.), your governance model is not simply corporate governance.

It spans multiple layers:

Corporate Governance
Technical Governance
Operational Governance
Research Governance
AI Governance
Quantum Governance
Ecosystem Governance
Federation Governance

So this document should become the master governance framework for the enterprise.
