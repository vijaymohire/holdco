**Status:** Work in Progress

---

# Overview

Enterprise Capability Architecture (ECA) defines the reusable business, engineering, operational and technology capabilities that collectively form the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Unlike organizational charts or software architectures, Enterprise Capability Architecture focuses on *what the enterprise is capable of doing* rather than *how it is implemented*.

Capabilities provide a stable architectural foundation that remains consistent even as repositories, technologies, organizational structures and implementation approaches evolve.

---

# Purpose

Enterprise Capability Architecture provides a common capability model for:

- Enterprise Architecture
- Repository Architecture
- Enterprise Workspaces
- Enterprise Bootstrap
- Enterprise Operating Platform
- Products and Services
- Subsidiaries
- Research Programs

- Customer Deployments

Every enterprise asset should implement one or more enterprise capabilities.

# Why Capability Architecture?

Enterprise structures change over time.

Repositories evolve.

Products change.

Technologies mature.

However, enterprise capabilities remain relatively stable.

Enterprise Capability Architecture enables long-term architectural consistency independent of implementation technologies.

# Capability Principles

Enterprise Capability Architecture follows several principles.

## Stable by Design

Capabilities remain stable while implementations evolve.

---

## Technology Independent

Capabilities describe business and engineering functions rather than implementation technologies.

---

## Reusable

Capabilities should be reusable across subsidiaries, products and customer environments.

---

## Composable

Capabilities may be combined to create larger enterprise solutions.

---

## Discoverable

Capabilities should be registered through enterprise metadata and registries.

---

## Governed

Every capability operates under enterprise governance.
