# Key Architectural Decisions

Sprint 4 introduced several important architectural decisions.

## Enterprise Ownership Model

The architecture now explicitly distinguishes:

- Owned Enterprise Domains
- Federated Operational Ecosystems

This became one of the fundamental principles of the enterprise architecture.

---

## Enterprise Federation

The Enterprise Federation Model established collaboration across:

- Universities
- Governments
- Industry
- Digital Valleys
- Smart Cities
- National Programs
- International Partners

while preserving enterprise ownership and digital sovereignty.

---

## Enterprise Factory

The Enterprise Factory was established as the primary enterprise engineering environment supporting:

- Product Foundry
- QAI Laboratory
- Technology Transfer Office
- Engineering Centers
- Working Groups
- Enterprise Operations

---

## Enterprise Operating Platform

The Enterprise Operating Platform evolved into the enterprise orchestration platform responsible for coordinating owned and federated operational domains through reusable enterprise capabilities and multi-control-plane architecture.

# Lessons Learned

Key lessons from Sprint 4 include:

- Enterprise architecture should evolve incrementally.
- Existing enterprise assets should be preserved whenever possible.
- Federation is preferable to centralized ownership for regional and global ecosystems.
- Enterprise capabilities provide a better abstraction than individual projects.
- Architecture should guide implementation rather than follow it.
