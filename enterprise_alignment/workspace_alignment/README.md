# Overview

The Workspace Alignment workspace defines how Enterprise Workspaces are organized, composed and aligned within the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Enterprise Workspaces provide the primary engineering environments used for research, architecture, engineering, product development, operations, education and customer solution delivery.

Rather than functioning as isolated development environments, Enterprise Workspaces compose repositories, enterprise capabilities and operational assets into reusable engineering platforms.

Workspace Alignment establishes a consistent architectural model for organizing these engineering environments across the enterprise.
