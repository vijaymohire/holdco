# Overview

The Technology Transfer Office (TTO) is responsible for transforming enterprise research, innovation and intellectual property into sustainable business value.

The TTO provides the organizational bridge between research, engineering, product development, commercialization and external collaboration.

Rather than operating solely as a patent office, the TTO coordinates the complete technology transfer lifecycle across the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Architectural recommendation

I also think the Technology Transfer Office (TTO) should eventually be recognized as one of the core functions within the Enterprise Factory, rather than a standalone administrative office.

A structure like this would fit naturally with the architecture we've been developing:
```
Enterprise Factory
│
├── Factory Governance
├── Corporate Affairs
├── Product Foundry
├── QAI Laboratory
├── Applied Research Center
├── Technology Transfer Office (TTO)
│      ├── Innovation Assessment
│      ├── Intellectual Property
│      ├── Patent Portfolio
│      ├── Licensing
│      ├── Commercialization
│      ├── Industry Partnerships
│      └── Startup & University Collaboration
├── Engineering Centers
├── Validation Centers
├── Working Groups
├── Knowledge Management
└── Enterprise Operations
```
This placement reflects the role you've described throughout the architecture: the Enterprise Factory is the owned operational hub, while the TTO acts as the gateway that moves validated research into products, services, partnerships and broader federated ecosystems. It complements the Product Foundry rather than overlapping with it, creating a clear innovation pipeline from research through commercialization.
