# Overview

Enterprise Resource Platform (ERP) Mapping defines how existing enterprise resource management capabilities are aligned with the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Rather than implementing a standalone Enterprise Resource Planning system, HoldCo adopts a broader Enterprise Resource Platform approach in which business operations, engineering, research, innovation and enterprise governance are coordinated through the Enterprise Operating Platform (EOP).

Existing ERP-related assets become reusable enterprise capabilities integrated into the Master Enterprise Reference Architecture (MERA).
