# Overview

Research Mapping defines how existing and future research assets are discovered, classified, aligned and integrated into the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Over time, numerous research activities have been conducted across GitHub repositories, GitLab workspaces, applied research programs, proof-of-concepts, validation laboratories and engineering initiatives.

Rather than treating these activities as isolated projects, HoldCo aligns research assets into a unified enterprise innovation pipeline that supports Enterprise Capabilities, Enterprise Products, Enterprise Services and the Enterprise Operating Platform.

Research becomes a reusable enterprise asset rather than a collection of independent experiments.
