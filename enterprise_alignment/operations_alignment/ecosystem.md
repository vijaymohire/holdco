# Overview

The Enterprise Operational Ecosystem defines how enterprise capabilities operate across distributed organizational, geographical and technological environments.

Unlike traditional enterprise architectures that focus on a single organization or data center, the HoldCo Operational Ecosystem supports interconnected enterprises, subsidiaries, factories, research centers, digital valleys, smart cities, autonomous zones and global Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) infrastructures.

The ecosystem provides the operational environment where Enterprise Capabilities, Enterprise Products, Enterprise Services and Runtime Frameworks collaborate through Enterprise Operating Platform (EOP).

The previous document (operations_alignment/README.md) defined what Enterprise Operations are.

This document should define what the Operational Ecosystem looks like.

Looking back over your previous GitLab workspaces (Factory, Valley, City, FAEP Ecosystem, Autonomous Zones, Control Planes, Fabrics, Enterprise Operating Platform), I think this document should become the master operational topology of the entire HoldCo ecosystem.

One important architectural distinction I'd introduce is:

Enterprise → Organizational hierarchy
Workspace → Engineering hierarchy
Runtime → Execution hierarchy
Ecosystem → Operational hierarchy

That keeps every abstraction clean.
