# Overview

Operations Alignment defines how operational capabilities are organized, coordinated and governed across the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem.

Rather than treating operations as a post-deployment activity, HoldCo views operations as a strategic enterprise capability that continuously manages enterprise platforms, products, services, research environments and Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) ecosystems.

Operations Alignment establishes a common operational architecture that connects Enterprise Frameworks, Enterprise Operating Platform (EOP), Runtime Frameworks, Enterprise Products and Enterprise Services into a unified operational model.

The next logical layer is Operations.

This is where your architecture becomes quite different from traditional enterprise architectures. From all the GitLab workspaces you've shared (Factory, Valley, City, Ecosystem, Control Planes, Runtime, Fabrics), operations are a first-class enterprise capability, not simply IT operations.

One distinction I'd establish immediately is:

Products deliver capabilities.
Operations sustain, orchestrate and evolve those capabilities.
