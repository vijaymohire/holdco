# Overview

The Digital Valley represents a federated regional innovation ecosystem within the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Architecture.

Unlike an enterprise or subsidiary, a Digital Valley is **not owned by HoldCo**.

Instead, it provides a collaborative environment where governments, universities, research organizations, startups, industry partners and enterprises cooperate through shared enterprise capabilities, engineering platforms and Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) technologies.

The Digital Valley provides the regional operational layer that connects research, innovation, engineering and commercialization.

# Enterprise Federation Model

The Digital Valley follows the Enterprise Federation Model (EFM).

Rather than belonging to a single organization, the Valley is composed of independently governed participants collaborating through common architectural principles.

Typical participants include:

- HoldCo
- Universities
- Research Organizations
- Government Agencies
- Industry Partners
- Startups
- Investors
- Customers
- Standards Organizations
- International Partners

Every participant retains ownership of its own assets while contributing to shared innovation objectives.
