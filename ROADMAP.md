# HoldCo Roadmap

> **Strategic Roadmap for Enterprise Organization, Bootstrap, Operations and Ecosystem Evolution**

---

# Overview

The HoldCo repository represents the enterprise composition layer of the ecosystem.

This roadmap outlines the progressive evolution of the HoldCo from an organizational reference repository into a reusable enterprise operating platform capable of supporting multiple subsidiaries, products, regions, partners and customer ecosystems.

The roadmap is organized into capability phases rather than calendar dates, allowing the architecture to evolve incrementally.

---

# Vision

Develop a reusable enterprise platform capable of:

- Building new organizations
- Bootstrapping subsidiaries
- Accelerating customer deployments
- Managing enterprise operations
- Scaling globally
- Supporting Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI ecosystems
