# Enterprise Hierarchy

> **Reference Guide for Enterprise Views, Hierarchies and Organizational Relationships**

---

# Overview

The HoldCo Enterprise Hierarchy provides a collection of architectural views that describe how the enterprise is organized, deployed, operated and evolved.

Rather than relying on a single organizational chart, the HoldCo architecture uses multiple complementary hierarchy views. Each view focuses on a specific aspect of the enterprise while remaining part of a unified Enterprise Operating Platform.

This approach enables architects, engineers, operations teams and business stakeholders to understand the enterprise from perspectives relevant to their responsibilities.

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Hierarchy provides reference models for:

- Enterprise Organization
- Operational Domains
- Enterprise Bootstrap
- Enterprise Operating Platform
- Deployment Architecture
- Control Architecture
- Ecosystem Relationships
- Future Enterprise Expansion

Together these hierarchy views describe how the HoldCo ecosystem is composed and operated.

---

The Enterprise Hierarchy is organized into several complementary views.

# Why Multiple Hierarchies?

Large enterprises cannot be accurately represented by a single hierarchy.

Different stakeholders require different architectural views.

Examples include:

- Executive Leadership
- Enterprise Architects
- Platform Engineers
- Product Teams
- Operations Teams
- Customers
- Partners

Each hierarchy provides information relevant to a particular audience while remaining consistent with the overall enterprise architecture.

# Design Principles

The Enterprise Hierarchy follows several principles.

## Multiple Views

No single hierarchy can describe the complete enterprise.

---

## Separation of Concerns

Each hierarchy focuses on one architectural responsibility.

---

## Reusability

Hierarchy models are reusable across subsidiaries and customer deployments.

---

## Scalability

The hierarchy supports organizations ranging from startups to global enterprises.

---

## Adaptability

Enterprise structures evolve while preserving architectural consistency.
