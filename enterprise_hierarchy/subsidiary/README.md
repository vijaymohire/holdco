# Subsidiary

> **Autonomous Enterprise Capability within the HoldCo Ecosystem**

---

# Overview

A subsidiary represents an autonomous organizational capability operating within the HoldCo Enterprise Operating Platform.

Each subsidiary specializes in a particular business domain while leveraging common enterprise services, governance, Enterprise Bootstrap and the Enterprise Operating Platform.

This architecture enables multiple organizations to innovate independently while remaining part of a unified enterprise ecosystem.

---

# Vision

Develop specialized organizations capable of delivering products, services, research and operational excellence while sharing reusable enterprise assets.

The subsidiary architecture promotes:

- Organizational autonomy
- Enterprise reuse
- Shared enterprise services
- Modular growth
- Continuous innovation
- Global scalability

---

# Design Principles

The subsidiary architecture follows several principles.

## Autonomous

Each subsidiary operates independently within its business domain.

---

## Composable

Organizations are assembled from reusable enterprise capabilities.

---

## Reusable

Enterprise assets are shared across subsidiaries.

---

## Configurable

Deployment profiles adapt subsidiaries to regional, customer and operational requirements.

---

## Software First

Capabilities are initially implemented through software-defined platforms before evolving into specialized infrastructure.

---

## Scalable

The architecture supports growth from a single business unit to multiple global organizations.

Subsidiaries represent autonomous enterprise capabilities operating within the HoldCo ecosystem.
