# Agriculture Post-Pilot — QAI Services

## Purpose

This directory defines the QAI service capabilities that can be offered to Agriculture clients following the pilot phase.

The service layer converts the Agriculture QAI architecture, products, engineering capabilities and validated technologies into structured client-facing services.

Services may combine:

- QAI products
- COTS components
- HoldCo Enterprise Library capabilities
- Agriculture-specific engineering
- Digital Twin
- Edge
- AI / QAI
- Communication
- CPS
- Validation
- Research capabilities

# Service Principle

A service is the operational delivery of one or more capabilities.

```text
Capability
    +
Product
    +
Engineering
    +
Deployment
    +
Operation
    +
Support
    ↓
QAI Service
```

---

# Product vs Service

```text
PRODUCT
Reusable Capability
      ↓
Software / Platform
      ↓
Defined Interface

SERVICE
Product
      +
Engineering
      +
Configuration
      +
Deployment
      +
Operation
      +
Support
```

A product may be delivered independently, while a service may combine multiple products and engineering capabilities.

# Governing Principles

## Client Value Before Technology

A service should solve a meaningful Agriculture problem rather than introduce technology for its own sake.

## Product-Service Separation

Products and services should remain architecturally distinguishable.

## Evidence Before Claims

Service benefits should be measurable.

## Baseline Before QAI

QAI services should use appropriate classical baselines.

## Safety Before Automation

Physical actions remain behind policy and safety boundaries.

## Local Resilience

Critical functions should support appropriate local operation.

## Security by Design

Identity, access, network and data security are service concerns.

## Data Sovereignty

Client data movement and processing remain policy-controlled.

## Interoperability

Services should integrate with existing client infrastructure.

## No Vendor Lock-In

Services should preserve reasonable technology and component interchangeability.

## Progressive Adoption

Clients should be able to adopt capabilities incrementally.

## Progressive Autonomy

Automation should increase only with validation and evidence.

## Research Discipline

Emerging quantum, photonic and related technologies remain explicitly classified until validated.

## Lifecycle Thinking

Services should include operation, maintenance, upgrade and retirement considerations.

---

# Governing Statement

> **The Agriculture Post-Pilot QAI Services layer converts validated and emerging Agriculture capabilities into structured client services covering assessment, architecture, sensing, Edge, Digital Twin, AI/QAI, optimization, CPS integration, validation, security, data governance, operations and managed intelligence. Services may combine QAI products, COTS components and engineering capabilities while maintaining clear boundaries between operational, development and research technologies. The service model supports incremental client adoption, measurable value, progressive automation, local resilience, security, sovereignty and evidence-based evolution toward intelligent and adaptive Agriculture.**
