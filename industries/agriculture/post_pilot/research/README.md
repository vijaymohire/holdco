# Agriculture Post-Pilot — Research

## Purpose

This directory defines the Agriculture Post-Pilot research layer.

The research layer provides a controlled path for investigating emerging technologies, algorithms, architectures and capabilities that may eventually contribute to Agriculture products, services or future deployments.

Research is explicitly separated from operational and validated capabilities.

# Governing Principles

## Research Before Productization

Interesting technology should be investigated before being represented as a product.

## Baseline Before Advantage

A relevant baseline must exist before improvement claims are made.

## Evidence Before Claims

Research conclusions should be supported by measurable evidence.

## Simulation Before Risky Deployment

Simulation and controlled experimentation should be used where appropriate before field deployment.

## Safety Before Autonomy

Research involving physical control must maintain safety boundaries.

## Human Oversight

High-impact experimental control should retain appropriate human oversight.

## Technology Neutrality

Research should evaluate technologies according to Agriculture requirements.

## Hardware Independence

QAI research should avoid unnecessary dependence on a single hardware implementation.

## Reproducibility

Research should preserve sufficient information to reproduce important results where practical.

## Provenance

Experiments and results should remain traceable.

## Data Governance

Research data should have defined access, storage and transfer controls.

## Security

Research systems must still protect devices, data and execution environments.

## Sovereignty

Data and operational boundaries should remain enforceable.

## Progressive Maturity

Research should progress through explicit maturity stages.

## Separation of Concerns

Research, development and operational capabilities should remain clearly classified.

## Reuse

Validated research should feed reusable enterprise and Agriculture capabilities.

## Architecture Alignment

Research should remain connected to the HoldCo Enterprise Architecture.
