# Phase 12 — Commercialisation & Post-Pilot Readiness

## 1. Phase Identity

Phase: 12

Name: Commercialisation & Post-Pilot Readiness

Pilot: QAI Digital Farm

Parent Path:

industries/agriculture/digital_farm/profiles/pilot/phase_12

Primary Objective:

Complete the pilot by recording the validated work, registering reusable
knowledge and assets, creating a client-selectable delivery package model,
and defining optional downstream commercial and Post-Pilot pathways.

---

## 2. Purpose

Phase 12 is the final phase of the recommended 12-phase pilot lifecycle.

It converts the accumulated pilot work into a structured set of:

- evidence
- results
- models
- datasets
- notebooks
- configurations
- documents
- images
- architecture
- knowledge
- reusable assets
- delivery packages
- Post-Pilot candidates

The phase creates a reusable client delivery template.

It does not require every client to enter a TTO, patent, Foundry or SaaS
process.

---

## 3. Governing Principle

Complete the pilot.

Record the work.

Register the assets.

Let the client select the required deliverables.

Provide a controlled path to optional commercial and Post-Pilot activities.

Do not force downstream business processes that the client has not selected.

---

Phase 12 is the completion and packaging boundary of the pilot.
