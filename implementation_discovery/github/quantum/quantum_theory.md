Purpose:
Capture theoretical foundations that may support QAI products, algorithms,
architectures or future research.

Repository evidence indicates that the historical `quantum_theory` folder was
created specifically for:

    Quantum theories
    Quantum principles
    Quantum mathematics

The folder contains primarily educational, lecture, reference and research
material. Therefore the majority of assets should be classified as:

    EDUCATIONAL
    RESEARCH REFERENCE
    THEORETICAL FOUNDATION

with selected materials potentially serving as:

    ENGINEERING FOUNDATION
    DESIGN INPUT
    VALIDATION INPUT
    REUSABLE KNOWLEDGE ASSET

Repository evidence:

    quantum_theory/readme.txt
        "For All Quantum theories, principles and maths"

The parent repository is described as containing quantum work from partner
courses, academic training and self-study, with educational and demonstration
purposes. Therefore theoretical material should not automatically be treated
as implemented QAI technology.
