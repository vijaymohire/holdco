# Master Enterprise Reference Architecture

> **The Enterprise Reference Architecture for the HoldCo Digital Enterprise Ecosystem**

---

# Overview

The Master Enterprise Reference Architecture (MERA) defines the overall structure, composition and interaction of the HoldCo ecosystem.

It provides a unified architectural view that connects enterprise organization, knowledge assets, technology platforms, operational ecosystems, runtime platforms and customer solutions into a single reusable enterprise model.

Rather than representing a single software platform, MERA defines a Digital Enterprise Engineering Framework capable of supporting research, education, product engineering, enterprise operations and Hybrid Classical + Quantum AI (QAI) ecosystems.

---

# Vision

Create a reusable enterprise architecture capable of supporting:

- Holding Companies
- Subsidiaries
- Research Organizations
- Universities
- Innovation Centers
- Customer Ecosystems
- Digital Valleys
- Smart Cities

- Space Operations
- Future Quantum AI Enterprises
